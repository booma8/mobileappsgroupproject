## Ideas

Moon phases: tells the phases of the moon
Tap counter: tells you how fast you can tap the screen
Image getter: gets random stock images for inspiration

Card shuffler: shuffles a digital deck of cards for use
Random music: randomly makes different pitches to make a melody out of
Name generator: makes a random name using sylables from a selected language

Fingernail timer: times how long it would take for fingernails to grow
Strobelight: flashes the screen different colors to create a strobe effect
Box calculator: calculates how big a box needs to be to hold a number of items

Fake captcha: creates a fake captcha to solve as a time waster
Constelation list: a scrollable map of the stars
Book size generator: takes a number of words and calculates how big it would be as a book

## Top 3 via vote

Constelation list
Mobile: Unique, smoothly scrolling around a map of the stars could be soothing
Story: Interesting, it allows people to relax and look at stars
Market: Small, it is easy to look up certain constelations
Habit: Not very addictive, more of an encyclopedia
Scope: Relatively small, a big scrollable image with a high amount of areas you click on

Card shuffler
Mobile: Haptic, you use your finger to shuffle the cards like a real deck
Story: Ineffective, most people I know would rather use real cards
Market: Not as big, you wouldn't be able to play with the cards easily
Habit: Not at all, maybe a novelty for a few times
Scope: Incredibly small, all you need is the card images and a randomize function for an array

Moon phases
Mobile: Quick, allows you to quickly see what phase the moon should be
Story: People like looking at the moon, especially when they can't see it normally
Market: Medium, some people are really into the moon phases 
Habit: Maybe once a week if you forget, not super addictive 
Scope: Small, needs an API to double check if the phase is correct
